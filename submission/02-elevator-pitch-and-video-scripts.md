# Elevator pitch (2–3 sentences)

When NASA's fire record switches from MODIS to the more sensitive VIIRS sensors, a naively joined record shows a 72% per decade rise in US fire activity, and a harmonized record shows none. FireLens harmonizes 26 years of NASA FIRMS hot spots for the United States and Iberia into one outage-aware, MODIS-equivalent record, validated on held-out years. Researchers and land managers can see when their area burns, whether that has changed, how sure we are, and download an evidence-backed brief.

# Longer demonstration script (about 3 minutes)

1. **Problem (20 s).**
   - On screen: open the app on the whole US and switch the scale to "Raw fire-cell-days: VIIRS" and back.
   - Say: "VIIRS detects about twice as many fire-cell-days as MODIS for the same fires, and the MODIS satellites are drifting."
2. **Harmonized calendar (25 s).**
   - On screen: point out the calendar legend (dotted outline = estimated, partial month marked) and that November 2026 is incomplete.
   - Say: "The record is observed MODIS from 2002 to 2021 and estimated outside that, and every estimate carries a 90% interval."
3. **Discovery (40 s).**
   - On screen: open tab 3 "Why harmonization matters" for the US, then the Iberia case.
   - Say: "US: naive splice +72% per decade, harmonized about zero. Iberia: naive +36%, harmonized −35%. The sign reverses."
4. **Regional decision support: Texas (45 s).**
   - On screen: open the Texas case, which lands on Fire season.
   - Say: "March is the peak month in both periods. No change in timing or concentration survives the permutation test. April 2011 is the record month, and the measurement check shows VIIRS sees relatively more burning outside the March peak."
   - On screen: go to Documented fires.
   - Say: "Possum Kingdom, Bastrop, Smokehouse Creek, from Texas agencies. The largest fire by area is not a record month in fire-cell-days, because burned area and detected burning measure different things."
5. **Evidence (30 s).**
   - On screen: open Evidence lab.
   - Say: "Held-out MAE 482 against 9,694 for raw VIIRS. Interval coverage is measured out of sample. Small-area intervals were too narrow; we widened them and say exactly how that was validated."
6. **Brief (20 s).**
   - On screen: open Regional brief and download the HTML.
   - Say: "Planning context, never a forecast. Incidents, uncertainty, provenance hashes."
7. **Close (10 s).** Say: "Two regions, complete since 2000, and every number traceable to NASA FIRMS."
