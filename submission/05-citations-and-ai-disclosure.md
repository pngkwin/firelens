# Data citations, sources, acknowledgments and AI-use disclosure

## NASA data

We acknowledge the use of data from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS), https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/, part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). Near-real-time data are provided by LANCE.

Products used (science-quality archive, then near-real-time):

- **MODIS Collection 6.1 active fire / hot spots (Terra, Aqua):**
  - MCD14ML, doi:10.5067/FIRMS/MODIS/MCD14ML;
  - near-real-time MCD14DL, doi:10.5067/FIRMS/MODIS/MCD14DL.NRT.0061.
- **VIIRS 375 m active fire, S-NPP:** VNP14IMG(T), doi:10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VNP14IMGT_NRT.002.
- **VIIRS 375 m active fire, NOAA-20:** VJ114IMG(T), doi:10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VJ114IMGT_NRT.002.
- **VIIRS 375 m active fire, NOAA-21:** VJ214IMG(T). Confirm the DOI on the FIRMS citation page before submitting (see the checklist).

Additional US records:
- **Archive requests:** FIRMS archive request IDs DL_FIRE_M-C61_850633, DL_FIRE_SV-C2_850636, DL_FIRE_J1V-C2_850637 and DL_FIRE_J2V-C2_850638.
- **API downloads:** all other records came from the FIRMS area API.
- **Hashes:** SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the published manifests.

## AI-use disclosure

The team developed FireLens with Anthropic's Claude (in Claude Code) as an AI assistant. It was used for:
- writing and refactoring code, data-pipeline scripts and tests;
- running analyses and audits;
- looking up and checking sources;
- drafting documentation and these submission texts.

The team directed and reviewed the work. Every number shown in the app and in these documents is computed by the FireLens code from NASA FIRMS data and checked by automated tests; none was generated by the AI model. Fire-incident facts are quoted from the cited agency pages. No AI-generated imagery or synthetic data is used in the product (synthetic inputs appear only in unit tests).
