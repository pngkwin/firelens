# FireLens 30 s video plan

Angle: the naive satellite record says US fire grew 72% per decade; the harmonized record says 0%. FireLens is the tool that shows the difference and lets you check it.
Tone: polished. Forest and ember palette from the app, Source Serif 4 titles, Source Sans 3 text.
Music: 120 BPM, D minor (Dm, Bb, F, C), synthesized in `work/music.cjs`. Every cut lands on a beat.

| Time | Scene | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Hook | "+72%" in ember serif | per decade. That is the US fire trend when NASA's two satellite records are joined naively. |
| 3 to 6 s | Reveal | FireLens wordmark over the blurred calendar | NASA fire detections since 2000, on one consistent scale. |
| 6 to 11 s | Calendar | Real calendar, push in; then the month heat map | Every month since November 2000, for the United States and Iberia. |
| 11 to 16 s | Transition | Cursor opens step 3; both trend cards highlighted | Joined naively: +72% per decade. Harmonized: 0%. |
| 16 to 20 s | Texas | Cursor opens the Texas case; peak month finding highlighted | Texas: the March peak holds across both decades. |
| 20 to 24 s | Evidence | Evidence lab, then the Smokehouse Creek record | Validated on held-out years and against documented fires. |
| 24 to 27 s | Brief | Cursor clicks Download brief (HTML); download toast | A regional brief, ready to download and print. |
| 27 to 30 s | End | Wordmark, URL, scope line | firelens-iota.vercel.app |

All UI is captured from the running app (`work/capture.cjs`); numbers on screen are the app's own. No personal data appears.
